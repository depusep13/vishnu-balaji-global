About Vishnu Balaji Global

Vishnu Balaji Global Imports & Exports connects international buyers with agricultural products sourced directly from India. We supply premium-grade 1121 Basmati Rice, Non-Basmati Rice, Milling Wheat, Pigeon Pea (Toor Dal), Pure Turmeric, Dry Red Chilli, Millets, Indian Spices, Maharashtra Onions, and Fresh Tender Coconuts to wholesale distributors, supermarket chains, and food manufacturing enterprises worldwide.

Backed by direct procurement channels from growers across Punjab, Haryana, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Andhra Pradesh, we ensure consistent physical quality, standardized sizing, low moisture thresholds, and export-compliant certifications. We handle end-to-end export coordination, including private labeling, customized pack sizes, phytosanitary clearances, pre-shipment inspections, and port customs logistics from Mundra, JNPT Nhava Sheva, Chennai, and Kakinada ports.
