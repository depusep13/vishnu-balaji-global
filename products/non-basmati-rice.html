Variety Overview & Global Demand

India is the world's foremost supplier of non-basmati rice, feeding millions across West Africa, East Africa, the GCC, and Southeast Asia. Non-Basmati Rice is prized for its high nutritional density, resilient cooking tolerance, versatile starch characteristics, and cost-effective bulk pricing.

At Vishnu Balaji Global, we procure directly from established milling clusters in the Krishna-Godavari delta of Andhra Pradesh and the central rice-bowl belts of Chhattisgarh. Every consignment undergoes mechanical pre-cleaning, de-stoning, rotary grading, and precision optical Sortex separation to deliver clean, uniform, and pest-free grain manifests.

Key Export Varieties

  • IR64 Parboiled Rice (5% / 25% Broken): Long-grain parboiled rice widely imported by West and East African nations (Benin, Togo, Senegal, Kenya, Somalia) due to its firm grain texture and non-sticky cooking profile.
  • IR64 Raw Rice: Natural unsteamed white rice suitable for retail distribution and staple food programs.
  • Sona Masoori: Medium-grain aromatic rice popular across diaspora communities and South Indian restaurants globally, known for its light texture and easy digestibility.
  • Swarna & PR11: Economical short/medium grain rice used extensively for food security distribution, humanitarian shipments, and institutional catering.
  • 100% Broken Rice: Graded, sortex-cleaned broken rice utilized for beer brewing, rice flour milling, animal feed formulations, and starch production.

Packaging & Vessel Logistics

We facilitate standard 25kg and 50kg PP woven sacks, with or without inner polyethylene liners, engineered to withstand long maritime voyages and tropical humidity. Sacks are stitched with heavy-gauge synthetic thread and labeled with customer marks, production/expiry dates, and destination country regulatory notices.

  • 20ft Containerized (FCL): 25 to 26 Metric Tons per container (stuffed floor-loaded or palletized with container desiccants).

Importer FAQs

What is the Minimum Order Quantity for Non-Basmati Rice?
Our standard containerized MOQ is one 20ft FCL (approx. 25-26 MT). For break-bulk shipments, minimum consignment size is 3,000 MT.
Do you offer third-party inspection reports prior to sailing?
Yes, SGS, Intertek, or Bureau Veritas inspections are routinely arranged at the loading port to verify weight tallies, bag condition, broken percentage, and container hygiene.
