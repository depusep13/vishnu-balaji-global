Commodity Overview & Flour Characteristics

Indian wheat is recognized globally for its high grain hardness, golden vitreous appearance, low moisture content, and high water absorption capacity. Produced under intense sunlight during the grain filling stages, wheat grown in central and northwestern India delivers robust gluten matrices, making it highly demanded by industrial flour millers, pasta makers, biscuit factories, and international government procurement agencies across the Middle East, North Africa, and Southeast Asia.

Vishnu Balaji Global sources directly from prime grain mandis in Madhya Pradesh (Sehore, Ujjain, Vidisha) and Punjab. We enforce rigorous mechanical cleaning, air screen grading, and destoning to eliminate weed seeds, chaff, and inorganic matter, yielding a pure, high-density grain manifest with consistent test weights (hectoliter weight 77–80 kg/hl).

Available Varieties

  • Milling Wheat (Grade-A): Versatile bread and general-purpose flour wheat with balanced extensibility and resistance. Ideal for loaf bread, flatbreads, and confectionery.
  • Durum Semolina Wheat (Malwa Gold): Exceptionally hard, golden-amber vitreous grain with high yellow pigment content. Ideal for premium semolina (suji/rava), macaroni, pasta, and couscous manufacturing.
  • Feed Wheat: Standardized sound wheat grain formatted for commercial livestock and poultry feed formulations, offering high digestible energy.

Importer FAQs

What is the Minimum Order Quantity for Indian Wheat?
Containerized orders require a minimum of two 20ft containers (approx. 50 MT). Bulk vessel shipments require a minimum parcel of 5,000 MT.
How is fumigation handled for international bio-security?
All containers undergo certified Methyl Bromide or Phosphine fumigation under the supervision of accredited pest control officers, with official Plant Quarantine Phytosanitary endorsements issued prior to sailing.
