Produce Overview & Beverage Value

The Pollachi region of Tamil Nadu is globally acclaimed for producing India's sweetest, most voluminous green tender coconuts. Nourished by sub-surface Western Ghats aquifers and year-round tropical sunshine, Pollachi Tender Coconuts are naturally loaded with potassium, magnesium, calcium, and bioactive enzymes. They represent the ultimate clean-label functional beverage demanded by luxury hotels, juice bars, retail hypermarkets, and healthy lifestyle brands across the GCC, Europe, and Asia.

Vishnu Balaji Global harvests coconuts at peak 7-month maturity when water volume is maximal and the internal kernel is thin, translucent, and jelly-soft. Within hours of harvesting, nuts are sorted, calibrated by weight, trimmed into diamond shapes or semi-husked formats, and pre-cooled to initiate unbroken cold-chain stowing.

Available Export Formats

  • Diamond-Cut Trimmed Coconut: Husk is sculpted into a clean flat-bottom hexagonal diamond profile, shrink-wrapped in protective film with easy-opening straw guides. Ideal for luxury hospitality, retail supermarkets, and high-end cafes.
  • Semi-Husked Fresh Coconut: Fibrous outer green husk is partially pared down, maintaining protective natural insulation while reducing ocean freight weight.
  • Fully Husked Brown / Mature Coconut: De-husked solid brown shell coconuts for copra processing, desiccated coconut grating, and coconut milk extraction.

Packaging & Reefer Container Load

Trimmed coconuts are packed 9 to 12 nuts per ventilated master corrugated carton. Semi-husked nuts are packed 25 nuts per breathable PP mesh sack. Shipped in 40ft High Cube refrigerated containers holding approximately 14,000 to 18,000 nuts per container. Loaded via Tuticorin, Cochin, or Chennai ports.
