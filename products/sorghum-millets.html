Ancient Supergrains: Global Sourcing

Following the United Nations declaration of the International Year of Millets (IYM), Indian millets have surged to the forefront of the global functional foods market. Packed with dietary fiber, calcium, iron, and a low glycemic index (GI), Sorghum (Jowar) and Millets provide ideal raw materials for gluten-free bakeries, extruded snack foods, breakfast granolas, beer brewing, and livestock feed formulations.

Vishnu Balaji Global sources from dedicated drought-resilient agricultural zones in Maharashtra (Solapur, Marathwada), Karnataka (Bijapur), and Rajasthan. Our processing lines feature rotary drum cleaners, aspiration channels, destoners, and multi-channel optical sorters, ensuring consistent round grain geometry and uniform bushel weight.

Available Varieties

  • White Sorghum (Jowar / Maldandi): Creamy-white, large-seeded food-grade sorghum prized for non-bitter taste and light flour color.
  • Yellow / Red Sorghum: Cost-effective grain with high polyphenol and energy content, popular for animal and poultry feed.
  • Pearl Millet (Bajra): Small, greenish-grey round grains high in iron and zinc, widely utilized for flatbreads, extruded snacks, and high-protein feed.
  • Finger Millet (Ragi): Brick-red micro-grains with the highest calcium concentration among all cereals (over 340mg per 100g).
