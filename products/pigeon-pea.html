Commodity Overview & Nutritional Profile

Pigeon Pea (Cajanus cajan), commonly recognized in international trade as Toor Dal, Arhar Dal, or Yellow Split Pigeon Peas, is a staple dietary pulse across the Indian subcontinent, the Caribbean, East Africa, and the Middle East. Rich in essential amino acids, dietary fiber, and complex carbohydrates, it serves as an indispensable ingredient in global plant-based protein diets, canned food processing, and traditional pulse cuisines.

Vishnu Balaji Global coordinates direct sourcing from prime dal milling hubs in Latur and Akola (Maharashtra) and the GI-tagged pulse plains of Kalaburagi (Karnataka). Our partner de-husking facilities utilize modern roller friction mills and multi-tier optical Sortex graders, ensuring that each batch is free of stones, weevils, dust, and immature grains.

Available Processing Types

  • Unpolished Yellow Split Toor Dal: 100% natural split peas without added moisture or oil coatings, favored by health-conscious food distributors and organic grocers.
  • Oily Polished Toor Dal: Conditioned with food-grade edible castor oil film according to traditional preservation methods, providing superior protection against storage pests and moisture absorption during lengthy tropical sea voyages.
  • Whole Pigeon Pea (Arhar): Unsplit whole grain dried to uniform moisture thresholds, supplied in bulk to overseas processing plants and canned legume manufacturers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the shelf life of Indian Toor Dal?
When stored in dry, cool conditions below 22°C, unpolished Toor Dal retains optimum cooking quality for up to 18 months, while oily-conditioned Toor Dal can maintain stability for 24 months.
Can we get third-party laboratory Certificate of Analysis (COA)?
Yes, comprehensive laboratory COAs detailing aflatoxin levels, pesticide residues, heavy metals, and moisture percentages from NABL/SGS accredited testing laboratories accompany each export invoice.
