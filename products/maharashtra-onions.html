Produce Overview & Global Demand

The Nashik district of Maharashtra, anchoring the renowned Lasalgaon APMC market, is the global benchmark for fresh red onions. Cultivated in mineral-rich black volcanic soils, Maharashtra onions are characterized by tight multi-layered papery skins, high pungency, crisp flesh texture, and long storage dormancy that withstands international sea freight.

Vishnu Balaji Global manages dedicated sourcing directly at farm-gate collection centers across Nashik, Ahmednagar, and Pune. We supervise field drying and curing to ensure that the neck tissues are thoroughly sealed, eliminating mold proliferation and premature sprouting during transit.

Available Varieties & Seasons

  • Garwa Onion (Late Rabi / Summer Crop): Harvested March through May; known as the premier export onion with dense solid flesh, multiple outer skin layers, and 4 to 6 months natural ambient storage life.
  • Pol Onion (Kharif / Early Winter Crop): Harvested October through December; vibrant red color, juicy flesh, ideal for rapid reefer shipment across the Middle East and Southeast Asia.
  • Rangda Onion (Late Kharif): Harvested January through February; balanced sweetness and pungency.
