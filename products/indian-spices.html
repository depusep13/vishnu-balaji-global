Cumin, Coriander & Pepper

Commodity Overview & Aroma Profiles

India is recognized historically as the Spice Bowl of the World, supplying over 48% of global spice volume. Indian spices are defined by high essential oil concentrations, potent aromatic bouquets, and deep distinctive pungencies. They are fundamental inputs for global seasoning blenders, extraction chemists, industrial sausage makers, and commercial spice packaging brands.

Vishnu Balaji Global sources directly from prime regulated APMC spice trading mandis: Unjha (Gujarat) for Cumin, Ramganj Mandi (Rajasthan) for Coriander, and Bodinayakanur/Idukki for Cardamom and Pepper. We deploy magnetic separators, de-stoners, and multi-tier Sortex optical sorters to guarantee clean, foreign-seed-free bulk consignments.

Core Spice Portfolio

  • Cumin Seeds (Cuminum cyminum / Jeera): Available in Singapore Quality (99% purity) and Europe Quality (99.5% Sortex cleaned). High thymol essential oil content (2.5% to 3.5% v/w).
  • Coriander Seeds (Coriandrum sativum / Dhania): Sourced in Eagle, Scooter, and Single Parrot qualities. High linalool content with sweet citrusy aroma.
  • Black Pepper (Piper nigrum): Tellicherry Garbled Special Extra Bold (TGSEB 4.75mm+) and standard 500GL to 550GL density grades with high piperine pungency.
  • Green Cardamom (Elettaria cardamomum): Alleppey Green Extra Bold (AGEB 7mm to 8mm+) with intense cineole aroma and vibrant natural green pods.
