Core Commodities Shipped to the UAE

  • 1121 Sella & Steam Basmati Rice: 8.35mm+ extra long grain, double Sortex cleaned, packed in 5kg, 10kg, 20kg, and 40kg master bags for supermarket retail and catering.
  • Guntur Teja S17 & S4 Sanam Dry Red Chilli: High pungency and vibrant red ASTA color, stem and stemless, packed in 10kg/25kg bags.
  • Pure Nizamabad Turmeric: Double-polished whole fingers with certified 3.5%–4.5% natural curcumin.
  • Nashik Fresh Red Onions: 45mm+ to 55mm+ diameter packed in 10kg/25kg ventilated mesh bags in 40ft refrigerated containers.

Customs Clearance Documentation Set

Every consignment dispatched to Jebel Ali includes:

  1. Commercial Invoice & Detailed Packing List
  2. Original Clean On-Board Ocean Bill of Lading (B/L)
  3. Certificate of Origin issued by authorized Indian Chamber / FIEO
  4. Official Phytosanitary Certificate issued by Ministry of Agriculture (India)
  5. Pre-shipment Inspection Certificate of Quality and Weight by SGS or Intertek
  6. Non-GMO Certificate and Fumigation Certificate
